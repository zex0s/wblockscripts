# wblockscripts

Userscripts for Tampermonkey. With Tampermonkey installed, click a script's install link and then Install.

## YouTube Preview Volume

Sets the volume of YouTube hover previews (home page, search, etc.). Default 15%; change it from the Tampermonkey menu on YouTube → **Set preview volume**.

Install: [youtube-preview-volume.user.js](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/zex0s/wblockscripts/main/youtube-preview-volume.user.js)

Port of [YouTube-Preview-Volume-Control](https://github.com/Master-Chief-Burner/YouTube-Preview-Volume-Control) by Master-Chief-Burner (MIT, see [LICENSE-YouTube-Preview-Volume-Control](LICENSE-YouTube-Preview-Volume-Control)).

## Hide X Username

Blurs your @username on x.com / twitter.com — in the account switcher (bottom-left) and on your profile page. Hover to reveal. Handy for screenshots and screen sharing. Your handle is detected automatically; mentions in tweets are left alone.

Install: [hide-x-username.user.js](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/zex0s/wblockscripts/main/hide-x-username.user.js)

## X Safari Back Fix

Fixes the X / Twitter feed going black in Safari after you open a tweet and go back, until you scroll. X restores your scroll position, but Safari keeps showing a stale one; the script does a quick jump to the top and back so Safari catches up.

Install: [x-safari-back-black-fix.user.js](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/zex0s/wblockscripts/main/x-safari-back-black-fix.user.js)

## ytpip

Adds a Picture-in-Picture button to the YouTube player controls (youtube.com and youtube-nocookie.com embeds). Click it to pop the video out; click again to bring it back.

Install: [ytpip.user.js](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/zex0s/wblockscripts/main/ytpip.user.js)

Based on [PiPifier](https://github.com/arnoappenzeller/PiPifier) (MIT).

## YouTube Disable Number Keys

Stops the 0-9 keys (and numpad) from jumping the video to 0%-90% when pressed by accident. Typing numbers in search, comments etc. still works.

Install: [youtube-disable-number-keys.user.js](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/zex0s/wblockscripts/main/youtube-disable-number-keys.user.js)
