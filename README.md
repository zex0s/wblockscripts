# wblockscripts

Userscripts for Tampermonkey.

## ytpip

Adds a Picture-in-Picture button to the YouTube player controls (youtube.com and youtube-nocookie.com embeds). Click it to pop the video out; click again to bring it back.

Install: with Tampermonkey installed, open [ytpip.user.js](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/zex0s/wblockscripts/main/ytpip.user.js) and click Install.

Based on [PiPifier](https://github.com/arnoappenzeller/PiPifier) (MIT).
